FILM EDITOR
Cut a film with taps. Open a cut (or start one), tap + to upload videos or photos from your phone — or the tray icon to bring in a whole Dump album — and they line up on the timeline. A photo is a still, held for four seconds. Play, or step with the arrows — ‹ › move one frame, « » one second. The piece under the playhead is always the selected one.

The tools work at the playhead: split cuts the piece in two, trim in/trim out move its start or end to where you are (on a still, that is how long it holds), earlier/later swap it with its neighbour, delete takes it off. Nothing touches the original footage — a trim can always be trimmed back out.

Sound is its own lane under the pictures. add sound lays a file under the film starting at the playhead; tap a sound bar and the same tools work on it — split, trim, nudge earlier/later by half a second, start here moves it to the playhead, delete. A selected sound also gets its level (−/+, a decibel a tap), fade in and out, mute, and ride — it rides the shot under its start, so it follows that shot wherever you move it. Tap the bar again to go back to the pictures.

The film icon top-left holds your finished films: Render bakes the cut into one real mp4 — free, on our own box — and every render is kept, yours and the chat's, newest first.
