Common questions

Do I need an account? No. The whole app works signed out — your readings, spells, Book of Shadows pages and School progress are saved right on your device. An account is only for syncing that content across your devices.

I signed in on a new phone and my things aren't there. Make sure you're signed in with the same method you used originally, then close and reopen the app — your saved content pulls down on sign-in.

Please delete my synced data. Write to us with the form above from the email address on the account and we'll remove it.

Are the readings real? They're written fresh for you, but they're for reflection, inspiration and entertainment — not advice about your health, money, or legal life.
