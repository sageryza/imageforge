PATTERN
Pieces — tap an animal or a fruit to put it in the pattern; tap again to take it out. Draw makes a new one in the fruit chart's look and costs about six cents.

Tile — one square that repeats. Drag a piece where you want it; the degrees box turns it, the size slider sizes it, again adds another of the same. Spacing grows the square under everything so the repeats land farther apart. Spin turns every piece a little, at random, within the degrees you set. A piece hanging off one edge comes back in on the other, which is what makes the repeat seamless.

Repeat — how it looks tiled. Grid, half-drop or mirror; the colour behind; Export renders one seamless PNG you can tile anywhere. Everything saves itself as you go.
