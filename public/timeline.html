STORY TIMELINE
Chats fill this shelf. Dictate a story's moments to any chat and it files them here; you put them in order.

Type a number to send a moment to that place. Single arrows move it one step, double ones send it all the way. In the gap between two moments: join them into one, or add a new one there. The mark under a number breaks a joined one apart.

The pencil on a card changes its words — and while it is open you can divide the moment at the cursor, or delete it. A unit of five or more folds to its first and last; tap a folded line to see the whole thing.

It saves as you go, and nothing here costs money.
