How it ships (the pattern)

One tile on the making home. It opens recordings from the audio drop and videos from the Dump — same room either way.
No double header. The native wrapper is the Episode Editor pattern: the app's own bar carries the tool title and THE back chevron, and this page header only ever names the recording. On the pick-a-recording list the page header folds away entirely.
The corner pill is the same shared autoscroll pill as Chats — the server injects the one source onto the page and the app's native pill is switched off, so it scrolls the page exactly like every other tool. The sideways one below only drives the playhead.
Everything is a tap. Nothing drags, scrubs or scrolls under your finger.

The sideways pill drives the playhead. ◀◀ rolls backward, ▶ plays, ▶▶ rolls forward. While it's moving, the ends turn into slower / faster (¼× up to 4×) and the middle stops it — the same five-speed grammar as the scroll pill in the corner.
Scissors drop a cut mark exactly where the playhead is. Get close at 1×, creep at ¼×, then tap.
Every mark splits the recording into pieces. Tap a piece on the strip to drop it (rose, struck); tap again to keep it.
In the Marks list, −.1 / +.1 nudge a mark by a tenth of a second, and tapping its time jumps the playhead there so you can listen across it.
Render makes one new file with the dropped pieces gone — the original is never touched.
