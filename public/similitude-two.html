Similitude, for two. Three triangle cards around a middle triangle, three more in your hand. On your turn: swap one card from your hand onto the board (tap a board card, then a hand card), pass, or tap Set! and say what the three share — that claims the set and scores it.

All the same — one thing all three share, written in the middle.
Each different — a fourth thing in the middle, and each card's own connection to it on the side it touches.
Model finds it — nothing to write; the model reads the three cards and draws the thing that connects them.

Draw it! makes a new card from a set you found — the picture that unites them — and lands it in that set's tile. About 2¢ a card, up to a dollar per person at a table. Challenge a set the other player found: if a card in your hand fits their rule, the set is yours.
