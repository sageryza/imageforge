Import art into the pipeline

Drop in card images you made elsewhere (e.g. bulk-downloaded from your own Midjourney). They're stored as a named, keyword-tagged batch that the deck workflow can pull from.

The download itself happens on your computer with a Midjourney export tool — this page picks up from there. Once uploaded, tell Claude the batch name and it can review the images, pick the on-style ones, and run them through print prep.
