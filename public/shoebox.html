SHOEBOX
Your Memory Library as polaroids, and your corkboards. LIBRARY is every memory — a pictureless one shows its words on undeveloped film. Open one to pin it to the board. On the BOARD, drag polaroids to arrange them, String ties constellations (tap cards one after another), Order numbers the play sequence, and ▶ Play glides from pin to pin — on the star paper the constellations light up at the end. Boards and everything you do here are the same boards as the Shoebox itself. Square it on a polaroid opens the Squaring tool.
