Similitude

drawing
Similitude. Three triangular cards around a middle triangle. Say what they have in common in the middle — or tap a card to swap it until you find a set.

All the same — all three share one thing; write it in the middle.
Each different — each card shares something different with a fourth thing; the middle names the fourth thing, and each card's connection is written on the side of the middle triangle it touches, venn style.
Model finds it — nothing to type: the three cards are shown to the image model with the rules, and it finds the connection itself (~5¢).

Found a set draws a brand-new card from your qualities — the center of the venn diagram. It appears upside down in the middle, joins the pool, and a tap on it deals the next hand. About 2¢ a card.
