Triset. Three triangular cards around a middle triangle. Say what they have in common in the middle — or tap a card to swap it until you find a set.

All the same — all three share one thing; write it in the middle.
Each different — each card shares something different with a fourth thing; the middle names the fourth thing, the three lines name each card's connection.

Found a set draws a brand-new card from your qualities — the center of the venn diagram — and it joins the pool. About 7¢ a card.
