ASSEMBLY
Put clips in order — get one film. Open an assembly (or start a new one) and the clips already placed ride the timeline along the bottom. Tap a clip on the shelf and a little place indicator appears in every gap on the timeline — tap the gap where it goes and it drops in between. Tap a clip already ON the timeline to pick it up: the same indicators move it, and the bar above the timeline plays it or takes it off (the clip itself stays on the shelf).

The round ▶ plays the arrangement clip by clip — a rough preview. Render bakes the real film: every clip on one canvas, sound intact, one mp4. Renders are kept newest-first above the shelf and nothing is ever overwritten. All of it is free — no model calls, just our own box.
