ASSEMBLY
Put pieces in order — get one film. An open assembly shows ITS OWN pieces: everything you've brought in, READY TO DROP IN, with the timeline along the bottom. Tap a piece and a little place indicator appears in every gap on the timeline — tap the gap where it goes and it drops in between. Tap a piece already ON the timeline to pick it up: the same indicators move it, and the bar above the timeline plays it or takes it off.

Three ways in: Upload opens your phone's own picker (photos and videos together, each arriving as it lands); From the Dump brings a whole already-dumped album, in album order; From the shelf opens the Chunking library — tap a clip there and it comes out in hand, ready to place. Nothing goes on the timeline until you put it there. A still sits on screen for the length you give it (pick it up on the timeline and tap 2s · 4s · 6s · 8s).

The round ▶ plays the arrangement piece by piece — a rough preview. Render bakes the real film: every clip and still on one canvas, sound intact, one mp4. Renders are kept newest-first above the shelf and nothing is ever overwritten. All of it is free — no model calls, just our own box.

The search speaks the house grammar: bare words need all of them, OR takes either, -word excludes, "quoted words" stay together, and tag: title: from: vo: kind: narrow a word to one field.
