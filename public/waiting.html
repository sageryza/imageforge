WAITING
What the “changes waiting” buzz is about. Everything merged that a deploy would change for you, under the chat that wrote it. Tapping a change opens its pull request. Merges that change nothing for you — notes, scripts, a Compare page that is already in your tab — sit shut under the list, and an iOS change waits on a build instead. Under that, the last five deploys — tap one to see what rode in it. Deploy at the top sends everything live — opens the deploy chat — say “deploy” there and it sends everything on this list live. If something is being drawn, the deploy waits for it by itself.
