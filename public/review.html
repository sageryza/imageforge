REVIEW QUEUE
Everything still waiting on your review — every swipe deck and comparison grid any chat has posted, with how far through it you are. Each tile is the first card of the content; tap it and you're straight on the cards. Your swipes save as you go, so a tile leaves this list by itself when you finish it.

Skip and Done are inside the deck — open one, tap Piles, and they're at the bottom, next to a link back to the chat that posted it. Skip means "not a review" (a browse deck, a demo) and moves it to a hidden pile behind DONE, where ↩ brings it back. Done means you're finished with it whatever the cards say.

AUTO is the ones nobody asked you for — the standing comparisons the server keeps by itself off the filed prompts and captions (same picture at two qualities, one style across different subjects). They appear and refresh on their own, so they sit in their own tab instead of on the pile a chat handed you.

Later stays counted as waiting — it's the pile you asked to come back to.
