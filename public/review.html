REVIEW QUEUE
Everything still waiting on your review — every swipe deck and comparison grid any chat has posted, with how far through it you are. Each tile is the first card of the content; tap it and you're straight on the cards. Your swipes save as you go, so a tile leaves this list by itself when you finish it.

Chats show up here too — tag a chat to be reviewed in the Chats app and it lands in this pile, with whatever it says it needs from you. The ✕ on a chat row takes that tag back off.

✕ means "not a review" — a browse deck or a demo you'll never swipe. It moves the tile to a hidden pile behind DONE, where ↩ brings it back.

Later stays counted as waiting — it's the pile you asked to come back to.
