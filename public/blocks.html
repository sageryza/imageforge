Cutting Blocks

Open a recording and it comes apart into lines.

Tap a line to open it — then a tap on any word cuts it in two there, the badge says who speaks it, and the number moves it. The ? marks it locked in or not sure; the ✕ puts it away.

The + adds a line you type, spoken in your voice. The chain joins two lines back into one. ▶ at the top plays the whole thing as you have marked it. Render cuts it for real.
