CHUNKING
Every reusable piece, on one shelf. A chunk is a named section of a finished video — footage and voiceover together — that you'd reuse whole somewhere else (the telepathy bridge, the manifestation trio). The smaller tiles are the atomic clips the films were cut from. Tap anything to play it, rename it, tag it, or leave a note — what you type is yours and never gets overwritten. HIDE takes one off the shelf; the hidden chip shows them again.

The search speaks the house grammar: bare words need all of them in one clip, OR takes either, -word excludes, "quoted words" stay together. Narrow a word to one field with tag: title: from: (the video it came out of) vo: (words in a chunk's voiceover) prompt: note: or kind: — e.g. kind:chunk telepathy.

New clips arrive by harvest, and chunks are filed by a chat naming a span of a finished video — both free. Ask any chat to chunk a section for you.
