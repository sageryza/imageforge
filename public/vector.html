VECTOR
Describe a few drawings — one per line, like a cat curled asleep inside an oversized teacup — and they come back as vectors: sharp at any size, and the outline is the cut line for a sticker. Already have a picture? Trace it instead — paste its web address, one per line: free. Colours can be changed after the fact by typing a name or a hex — also free, and the old version is never overwritten.

Flat ink-and-colour art only. A gradient or a photo has no flat colours to find and comes out bigger and worse than the picture you started with.
