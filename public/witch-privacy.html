Secretly a Witch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Secretly a Witch is a little app for daily tarot, moon phases, spells, dream illustration, astrology, and witchy learning. We built it to be delightful and to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

The short version: Most of what you make and save stays on your own device. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information. The only data that leaves your phone is what's needed to generate a reading or an image you asked for.

What stays on your device

Your saved readings, grimoire spells, journal entries, Book of Shadows pages, School progress, and preferences are stored locally on your device (in the app's own storage). We can't see them, and they aren't sent anywhere unless you choose to sign in to sync (below).

Optional sign-in & sync

If you choose to sign in with your email to sync your saved content across devices, we store your email address and the content you've saved so it can be restored on your other devices. Sign-in is entirely optional — the app works fully without an account. If you'd like your synced data deleted, contact us (below) and we'll remove it.

Features that use the internet

Some features send information to our server, and on to trusted AI providers, only to create the result you requested:

  • Daily reading, tarot & horoscope, spells, familiars: the words you enter and your selections are sent to generate a written reading.
  • Astrology / natal chart: the birth date, optional birth time, and birth city you enter are used to calculate your chart and reading.
  • Dream illustration & charm images: the text you write is used to generate an illustration; generated images may be stored on our servers so they can be shown back to you.

We use OpenAI to generate text and images. Your inputs are sent to them solely to produce your result and are handled under their terms. We don't send them your identity, contacts, or location beyond the birth details you type in for astrology.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.
  • No selling or renting of your personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or precise location.

The shop

Tapping through to the shop opens secretlyawitch.com, which is run on Shopify. Any purchase there is handled by that store and its own privacy policy, separate from this app.

Children

Secretly a Witch is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
