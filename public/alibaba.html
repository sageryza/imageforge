A fictional chat, drawn at real screenshot size — 1170 × 2532, the same pixels the phone writes.

In the script, them: starts a message from the supplier and me: starts one from you. A line with no prefix carries on the message above it, and a blank line inside a message is a paragraph break — that is how a long supplier pitch comes out in paragraphs. A line reading img: hangs the attached picture under that message.

Visible messages is which frame you are exporting; Play walks them on one at a time so you can screen-record the buildup.

Use invented names. The dramatization label is on by default.
