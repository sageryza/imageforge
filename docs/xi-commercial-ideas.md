## 1. "Susan, you're so warm" — the finished story

**The clearest pitch of the three, and it already ends on the product line.**
Her mom starts a story she has told a hundred times; Sophie finishes it for her
in a flat, bored voice, beat for beat. Mom: *"yeah, how'd you know?"* Sophie:
*"it's cuz I keep your stories in my memory library."*

Her own two versions, in the recording:

- The guy from El Salvador who slept on the porch all night — *"and you said,
  it's freezing outside, come inside."*
- The camping guy: the paint party, *"he rubbed paint on you and said, Susan
  you're so warm"* — *"but it turned out he was gay."*

Shape: two-hander on a couch, mom and daughter. The joke is the DEADPAN
interruption, so the performance carries it — the pill/infomercial framing
the other Xi ads use is not needed here.

## 2. "The stories people always tell" — the family table

From *Night ideas*. Same premise widened past her mom: everyone has the one
story they always tell.

- **Mommy's**: her dad leaving the business card on the table. She describes
  the shot herself — *"sitting at a fancy restaurant with a white tablecloth,
  all five of them, Mom, Dad, two girls, and a boy, the older girl is blonde,
  the younger one has short curly hair, and the boy is the youngest with really
  curly brown hair."*
- **Daddy's**: them knocking his head against the wall.
- **Charlie's**: grappling, going down the stairs — *"doesn't really make
  sense."* (Her words; the story is half-remembered on purpose.)
- **Hers**: unfilled — *"I'm not sure what story I always tell."* That gap is
  usable as the button: the app is what remembers hers.

This is a MONTAGE spot rather than a two-hander, and it wants illustrated
tableaux (the restaurant shot is fully art-directed above).

## 3. "Start over from square one" — HER VOICEOVER TAKE

**Not a third concept — it is a recorded VOICEOVER for one of the ads** (her
own correction, 2026-08-27: *"the video is a voiceover for one"*). The picture
is black for thirty seconds because she was recording audio; the take is the
whole file, performed, both voices.

> Breakups are hard, but you know what the worst part is? Having to start over
> from square one. So uh what's your favorite color? — Um sometimes I like
> purple but then on Wednesdays I kind of like green more, and then if it's a
> Tuesday kind of day, and then don't get me started on red.

So this is a **usable production asset**, not something to write: it is her
voice, her timing, her deadpan on the second half. The one thing NOT settled is
