Bright pasty afternoon sun outside a bar's wooden door. Thomas sits on the ground clutching his legs so that he resembles a ball, a vacant expression on his face. The girl comes out through the door into the sun and sits down next to him, her legs gently nudging his. Camera at eye level. In a high pitched, very detached voice, Thomas says: "I'm feeling extremely anxious." She looks at him, surprised.
