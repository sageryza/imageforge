Thomas is a boy with matted hair, a slender frame, gripping two bags, one canvas and one leather, both black. So pale he looks almost white; his size due not to his age but to lack of sustenance. Disheveled hair, dark smudges on his face, bags under his eyes, brown eyes. He speaks in a thin, tired drawl, slightly British. Wide eyes, always a little anxious, his brow often furrowed, mouth bent into a thin twist. A waffle weave shirt and black pants two sizes too large for him. The girl has gray eyes and matted, disheveled hair, and wears a flowered dress over striped leggings and mismatched socks.

Dusk at a park pond, ducks floating and quacking in slow circles on still water. Thomas and the girl sit side by side on the grass at the edge of the pond, looking out at the water, saying nothing, darkness slowly closing in on them. Camera at eye level. After a long quiet, Thomas says slowly, in his thin, tired drawl: "You seem to be... picking up on... all the signs you're given." The girl turns and looks at him.
