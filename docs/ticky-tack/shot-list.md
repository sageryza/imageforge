# Ticky Tack — the shot list (first pass, 2026-09-10)

Sophie's plan (2026-09-10): shoot the WHOLE script. The book is top-heavy
— most of the action is in the climax (Part III) — so the first scenes to
shoot are the climax (the pill · the bus · Alex's house), then the meeting
with Thomas, then the very beginning on the bus going the wrong way.

Her keeps, in her words: the car accident · the raw egg · the orange flags ·
her pretending to choke her (Thomas's wrists round her throat — short) ·
kicked out of Powell's (short but shot). Montage: sleeping in the jeep,
getting the books.

The book has ~75 scenes (68 `* * *` breaks over 40,000 words). Every line
below names the scene by the page's own words; the exact passage is in
`ticky-tack.md` beside this file.

## Shoot first — the climax (Part III)

1. The Rite Aid — Adam in the convertible with the prettier girl; the last
   pink box on a high shelf; tearing it open by the water fountain; the pill
   on her tongue while the girl in the grey vest stares; "God takes back";
   she swallows it dry; caught, the small scene at the counter, the $18.
2. The bus stop — free fries at McDonald's, she doesn't go in; the bus,
   four policemen board, "she's wearing a red dress," she glides out the
   door; the tan coat thrown down; the gravel; the stairs.
3. Alex and Alex — five brown doors, the last one opens; the closet full of
   soft clothes; "I was raped"; tea, the chihuahua, the Victoria's Secret
   postcard "You've reached the next level"; the silver car home.
4. Home — the tiny blue room, the foam slab; Jon's party downstairs; she
   gets up and goes back out for Thomas.
5. The reunion — Thomas hunched in the alcove outside Whole Foods, "Oh hi";
   the 44 back; her porch, Jon's friends ("are you guys friends of Jon's?");
   the cardboard over them under the window, the sliver of moon. "And then
   we take the 44 bus going the right way."

## Shoot next — the two beginnings

6. The very beginning — the 44 going the wrong way; alone under the two
   fluorescents; running down the winding road; the billboard "god takes
   back he who disobeys" (and the abortion one two metres on).
7. Meeting Thomas — the pale boy with two black bags; "Can I use your
   phone?" "No."; "Not one goddamn person"; "I've been thinking of ending
   things"; "me too, me too" under her breath; the intersection lights; the
   power box, eyes shut; his hand on her arm; under the tree, "I love you."

## Five moments with Thomas that must stay (mine)

8. The morning after — the man in the suit, the police car with no siren,
   "Homeless people are lazy," they walk off hand in hand.
9. The $5 dress — the woman with the crisp bill, the $5 bin, and Thomas
   pulls the black dress on, pale shoulders going pink in the sun.
10. The ducks — the pond at dusk, and the one line: "You seem to be…
    picking up on… all the signs you're given." Her favourite moment ever.
11. Ticky Tack — behind the metal box she finally pees; Thomas singing
    "little boxes" at the houses on the hill; the title, and the thesis.
12. Christmas morning — he tells her the image she said no to (the presents,
    the parents on the stairs, the gun); the afternoon goes grey.

## Hers, must stay

13. The choke — his wrists round her throat, "imagine this," no squeeze;
    the orange he doesn't share. Short.
14. Powell's — the bench, the bathroom mirror doll ("sign the bottom of my
    foot"), the man in the black vest: leave. Short.
15. The car accident — her arms piled with yard-sale things; his rant
    drilling into her; the crash; the teenager and the old couple; she
    leaves the rug and the string on the wall.
16. The raw egg — the man with the carton in front of the co-op; "You can
    eat them raw"; she does; the barrette and the lucky penny.
17. The orange trail — the crossing guard, the crows going every way; she
    switches from black to orange; priuses, shorts, socks; the orange flags
    behind the caution tape. "I had won."

## Secondary — recommend keeping

18. The hotel — the red hotel lobby, the fake room number, the black
    frozen couches, walking back out with the "luggage."
19. The voter man on the train — Thomas writes his name; she learns it.
20. The Winning Spot — "come back when you have money"; Thomas curled up
    as a ball, "I'm feeling extremely anxious"; the woman with the $5.
21. The free pizza — the bags checked with little white tags; the To Do
    List pad; the coke on the cement pillar and the paralysis in the lot.
22. The night she floats — Thomas asleep under his coat, she crosses the
    street like a ghost, the two men order her off, she runs back into his
    arms.
23. The coffee — the McDonald's coffee a woman hands her, Thomas dumps it
    in the bushes. "We have to get out of town."
24. The bus dare — the bar crawl of sips, the bus barrelling and she is
    told not to move; the wet leaves; Thomas thinks she was running off.
25. The leaving and the catching — she walks away at the co-op, he catches
    her under the bridge; the train tracks, "you're the one"; that night
    she slips out to her old house and takes the girl's underwear.
26. "Have a baby?" / "have sex?" on the yellow couch-thing.
27. The juice — stolen, the man's voice, the hill of trash: "It was worth
    it, though."
28. The split — the shelter, the bar and Mein Kampf, the fortune teller
    with the weed, she walks and doesn't wait; the scissors; the hoodlums
    under the awning; the lit-up alcove. (This is how Part III starts and
    can't be cut.)
29. The Black — the three on the train, the girl sobbing, the bathroom, the
    needle fourteen times; the curb she steps off and back onto; the man
    who leaves her at the fan; the pregnancy dawns. (Heavy; the whole of
    the red dress runs on it.)
30. The two Toms — the peanut butter aisle without him; the sous-chef;
    Thomas materialises; "I'll see you tomorrow."
31. The slap and the taxi — begging for $40, the boy hits her, the other
    gives three dollars; the taxi that dumps her by the freeway; the
    wheelchair; the gilded scrapbook, "Sophie's book of mistakes."
