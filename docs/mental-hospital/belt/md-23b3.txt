“Where did all my sculptures go!?” asks sophie, in a panic.   
there is a mexican cleaning lady, standing by the trash can with a mop, avoiding eye contact, pretending to be intent on her task.  
"esta es basura” says the lady.  
	“They’re not trash. They’re art.”  says sophie, shakily.
and when the lady still pretends not to notice sophie talking, she adds, as an afterthought: “And you’re a horrible person for throwing them away without telling me.” Sophie turns on her heel and marches back to her room.
