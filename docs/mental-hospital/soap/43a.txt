she mimes holding a heavy cup full of pills

Sophie takes the pill out of the cup and stares at it for a second. Then she puts the pill to her mouth but as she’s doing so, breaks off half of it in her hand. She swallows half of the pill and surrepititiously places the other half in her water cup.

“Veery Goood” says the nurse, smiling proudly, and Sophie beams back at her. The nurse takes both cups back and puts one inside the other.
