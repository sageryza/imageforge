That night Nurse Edna, the nurse in [Video2], walks into her room smiling and carrying the two cups: one with a pill, one with the water. “You’re lucky,” she says, continuing to smile at her though it’s now unmistakeable from a grimace. “Dr. Grayson haz onley geeven yo wan peel. Sahm of de oder patients take a whole cap full,” she mimes holding a heavy cup full of pills belonging to one of the other patients.

Sophie pulls the pill out of it’s resting place in the cup and stares at it for a lengthy pause. Then she puts the pill to her mouth but as she’s doing so, breaks off half of it in her hand. She swallows half of the pill and surrepititiously places the other half in her water cup.

“Veery Goood” says the nurse, smiling proudly, and Sophie beams back at her. The nurse takes both cups back and puts one inside the other.
