## BLAKE — the forest date

### Top moments

**1. The Fallen Tree**
- What happened: he gave her intentionally obscure directions to a fallen tree
  on a forest trail in downtown Portland, then sat on it doing nothing for the
  two hours she was lost on the wrong side of town.
- Caption: **The Fallen Tree** / *"I made him wait on some tree for 2 hours."*
  [trimmed — her sentence continues "after I took the bus to northeast instead
  of southwest"]
- Prompt: `Draw: a young man with long hair, in a punk t-shirt and jeans,
  sitting alone on a large fallen tree on a forest trail, doing nothing at all,
  looking content. No text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Inventions: "looking content" — journal says he texted that he was enjoying
  what he was doing in his tree.)

**2. The Directions**
- What happened: the rest of the directions lived on his OkCupid profile — go
  past three fallen trees and across two wooden bridges and you'll find him.
  She never found a single marker.
- Caption: **The Directions** / *"go past three fallen trees and across two
  wooden bridges, and I'd then find him."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: a winding forest trail with three fallen trees along it and
  two small wooden footbridges crossing it, a tiny figure of a young man seated
  on the farthest fallen tree. No text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Inventions: the single continuous view — the journal never sees all the
  markers at once; that is the point of drawing it.)

**3. The Watering Hole**
- What happened: after the lost morning she finally met him at the trail
  entrance and gulped from the water fountain with complete delight.
- Caption: **The Watering Hole** / *"I felt like a deer at a watering hole,
  which is something I feel like almost all the time anyway."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a petite young woman with curly brown hair drinking deeply
  and happily from a public drinking fountain beside a forest trailhead, a
  bench nearby. No text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Metaphor moment — can also be rendered the second way per the rubric: an
  actual deer drinking at a watering hole. Flag for Sophie; both would be kept.)

### Backups

**4. The Best Part** [composed title]
- What happened: on the bus home he was too self-conscious to talk in front of
  the other passengers, so they just sat in silence with his arm around her —
  and it was the nicest part of the date.
- Caption: **The Best Part** / *"we just sat with his arm around me without
  either of us talking much."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: a crowded city bus interior, a young man with long hair and a
  petite young woman with curly brown hair seated together in silence, his arm
  around her shoulders, other passengers all around. No text or lettering
  anywhere.`

**5. The Story Game**
- What happened: at the burger place they played a game writing parts of
  stories on the little pads of paper from her purse. She didn't eat most of
  her burger, and didn't let him pay for it.
- Caption: **The Story Game** / *"I didn't eat most of my burger."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: two burgers on a casual restaurant table, one barely eaten,
  with two little paper notepads and two pens lying between the plates. No
  text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Pairing: the pads and the burgers sat together in the scene.)

**6. The Wrong End of Town**
- What happened: she rode the sunny bus to entirely the wrong side of the city,
  and the panic when she realized was the most alive she'd felt in memory — she
  resolved, vaguely, to *do more things*.
- Caption: **The Wrong End of Town** / *"I couldn't remember the last time I
  felt alive like this."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a petite young woman with curly brown hair sitting alone on a
  city bus, morning sun slanting through the windows across the empty seats and
  a few other passengers. No text or lettering anywhere.`

### Timeline — short
bus (wrong way) → bus back downtown → the trail → water fountain → his fallen
tree → burger place → second bar → bus home → her bed → bus stop

### Timeline — long
bus to the wrong end of town (sun slanting through the windows; the panic;
"do more things") → back downtown, switch to the 4 (a gas station stop, walked
both directions before finding the trailhead) → the trail (hunting three fallen
trees and two wooden bridges, finding none; an Animal Collective break sitting

been on it two hours; the strangeness of meeting bypassed casually) → burger

arm-around ride) → her house (nap vs. cuddle argument; "was he ever going to
see me again" — no) → bus stop (she walked him there, waved when he got on)

### Statistics
- Duration: unstated (met around midday after her 2-hour delay; end unstated)
- Who paid: each their own at the burger place — she refused his offer; the
  second bar unstated
- Food & drink: a burger (mostly uneaten), unstated drinks at the second bar,
  water-fountain water
- Transport: four bus legs for her getting there (incl. the wrong-way ride),
  one bus home together, walking
- Locations: two buses, a forest trail in downtown Portland, a burger place, a

- Weird: hours he waited on the tree: 2 · wooden bridges promised: 2, found:

## LOUIS — the caturday date

### Top moments

**1. The Job Application**
- What happened: at Buffalo Exchange she applied for a job in the middle of the
  date, and he sat in the chair next to her waiting while someone came and
  interviewed her.
- Caption: **The Job Application** / *"he waited for me to fill out the
  application until someone came and interviewed me."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: inside a secondhand clothing store, a petite young woman with
  curly brown hair filling out paperwork at the counter while a young man
  waits in a chair beside her holding a small shopping bag, racks of clothes
  behind them. No text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Inventions: the shopping bag — journal says he bought something.)

**2. The Yoga Ball**
- What happened: he arrived, came inside, and casually started bouncing on her
  roommate's giant rubber yoga ball while she took the rolly chair.
- Caption: **The Yoga Ball** / *"he came inside and casually started bouncing
  on my roommate's giant rubber yoga ball."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a young man bouncing on a giant rubber exercise ball in a
  small living room, a petite young woman with curly brown hair watching him
  from a rolling desk chair. No text or lettering anywhere.`

**3. Breaking the Rules** [composed title]
- What happened: at Bossa Nova they danced a long while without him so much as
  grabbing her wrist, until she gave up waiting and put her hand on his hip.
- Caption: **Breaking the Rules** / *"I said fuck it and broke the rules,
  casually leaning my hand on his hip."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: a small crowded dance floor with colored disco lights set
  into the floor, a band of three elderly gentlemen playing on a small stage,
  a petite young woman with curly brown hair dancing with a young man, her
  hand resting on his hip. No text or lettering anywhere.`

### Backups

**4. Leggings**
- What happened: at the thrift store he tried on leggings — but first they
  tried on hats together.
- Caption: **Leggings** / *"he tried on leggings but first we tried on hats
  together."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a young man and a petite young woman with curly brown hair
  trying on hats together in front of a thrift store mirror, the man wearing
  leggings, racks of secondhand clothes around them. No text or lettering
  anywhere.`

**5. The Next Adventure**
- What happened: under the restaurant's jungle of ceiling-climbing vegetation
  he told her his next adventure: going to pick weed leaves and then take
  ayahuasca.
- Caption: **The Next Adventure** / *"he would be going to pick weed leaves
  and then take ayahuasca."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: a Mexican restaurant with extravagant green vegetation
  climbing to the ceiling, a young man and a petite young woman with curly
  brown hair at a small table sharing two different cocktails. No text or
  lettering anywhere.`
  (Pairing: the jungle plan told under the restaurant's own jungle.)

**6. Slow Breathing**
- What happened: after she said no he rolled over and slept completely
  separate from her, falling asleep quickly while she watched.
- Caption: **Slow Breathing** / *"he fell asleep quickly while I watched his
  slow breathing."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: a dark bedroom at night, a young man asleep on the far edge
  of the bed facing away, a petite young woman with curly brown hair lying
  awake beside him, a strip of empty sheet between them. No text or lettering
  anywhere.`
  (Inventions: "a strip of empty sheet between them" — journal says he slept
  completely separate.)

### Timeline — short
her house → Buffalo Exchange → Mexican restaurant → her house → Bossa Nova →
the car → her bed → morning

### Timeline — long

### Statistics
- Duration: start unstated ("caturday") through the next morning — an overnight
- Who paid: he paid for the restaurant cocktails ("I think"); club drinks
  unstated; he bought his own thrift purchase
- Food & drink: two different cocktails shared at the restaurant (food

- Locations: her house, Buffalo Exchange on Hawthorne, a Mexican restaurant, her

- Weird: jobs applied for mid-date: 1 · on-the-spot interviews: 1 · leggings

## ROBIN — the 4am date

### Top moments

**1. The Worst Eggs**
- What happened: the morning after, she made breakfast — the eggs all broke,
  burned black on the bottom, never got flipped, and were served on toast with
  the roommate's lettuce sprinkled on top.
- Caption: **The Worst Eggs** / *"He said they were the worst eggs he'd ever
  seen."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a plate holding two slices of toast topped with broken fried
  eggs burned black on the bottom, flecked with bits of charred crust and
  sprinkled with pieces of lettuce, on a small kitchen table. No text or
  lettering anywhere.`

**2. Four in the Morning** [composed title]
- What happened: back in her tiny bedroom she set up her little plastic horses
  and cows on a book and knocked them down with the pin from her toy bowling
  set.
- Caption: **Four in the Morning** / *"I set up my little plastic horses and
  cows on a book and used the bowling pin to knock them down."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: little plastic toy horses and cows standing on a hardcover
  book on a bedroom floor, some already knocked over, a petite young woman
  with curly brown hair sitting cross-legged holding a small toy bowling pin.
  No text or lettering anywhere.`

**3. The Six Pack**
- What happened: he arrived at 4am toting a six pack of cider; the implication
  hit her hard, she scooted away on the dark porch — and drank anyway.
- Caption: **The Six Pack** / *"I drank my first five sips in like one
  minute."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a dark porch at night, a petite young woman with curly brown
  hair sitting cross-legged a deliberate distance from a young man, a six pack
  of cider bottles between them, one open bottle in her hand. No text or
  lettering anywhere.`
  (Inventions: "a deliberate distance" — journal says she moved back from him.)

### Backups

**4. The Morning Report** [composed title]
- What happened: in the morning he announced he'd gotten a boner in the night
  and hadn't known what to do since she'd been asleep. She had a retort ready
  and didn't use it.
- Caption: **The Morning Report** / *"I left his foolish thought to hang in
  the air."* [trimmed — the retort she swallowed: "fat lot me being awake
  would've done"]
- Prompt: `Draw: morning light in a tiny bedroom, a young man sitting up in
  bed talking earnestly with his hands raised, a petite young woman with curly
  brown hair looking at him with a flat expression. No text or lettering
  anywhere.`

**5. The Co-Captain**
- What happened: a story he told from her bed — high school hockey, he and his
  co-captain had a thing, and he never really got over her.
- Caption: **The Co-Captain** / *"he thought about her all the time and wished
  that he could be with her."* [trimmed]
- Prompt: `Draw: two teenage hockey players in full gear standing side by side
  on an ice rink under arena lights, sticks in hand, seen from a distance. No
  text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Told-story moment — two levels from reality.)

**6. The Car Date**
- What happened: he told her about a date he'd been on the day before — he'd
  driven his friend Sarah somewhere and they'd had a date in the car. (This
  Sarah appears in person in the later robin/blake/david/sarah group entry.)
- Caption: **The Car Date** / *"I told him he shouldn't be giving people rides
  for no reason."* [verbatim]
- Prompt: `Draw: a small car parked on a quiet street, a young man in the
  driver's seat and a young woman in the passenger seat facing each other in
  conversation, seen through the windshield. No text or lettering anywhere.`
  (Told-story moment.)

### Timeline — short
4am porch → her bedroom → bed → morning kitchen → her bedroom → goodbye

### Timeline — long
