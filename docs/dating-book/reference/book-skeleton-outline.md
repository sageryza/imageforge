BOOK SKELETON OUTLINE

Fill this in as you go. Every piece has a place.

Book Structure

FRONT MATTER

Title Page

The Sophie Protocol — Introduction (becoming a blank slate, the rules of the experiment)

Hypothesis Page (what you expected to find)

THE DATES (Chronological)

Date 1: The Cuddle Convention — your birthday, Portland, a thousand consent rules, finding your first partner

Dates 2–50: Each date gets a spread (see page types below)

Essays are woven in between dates at natural breaking points

Infographics appear every 5–10 dates as palette cleansers

BACK MATTER

Conclusion / Data Summary — what Sophie learned (or didn’t)

Final infographic spread

Acknowledgments / About the Artist

Page Types

DATE SPREAD (2 pages per date): Left page = watercolor portrait + name/number. Right page = three small drawings of details you remember + brief text.

ESSAY PAGE (1–2 pages): Longer written piece on a theme, placed after a relevant date.

INFOGRAPHIC PAGE (1 page): Playful data visualization. Can be a full spread if needed.

CHAPTER OPENER (optional): If you want to group dates into loose acts (e.g. “Early Days,” “The Middle,” “Late Stage”).

Experiment Framing Notes

HYPOTHESIS: What did Sophie expect to find? This goes in the front matter.

DATA: Each date spread is a data point. The three drawings are the “findings.”

METHODOLOGY: The Sophie Protocol — no personality, no opinions, don’t end till they want to.

CONCLUSION: What patterns emerged? What did 50 data points reveal? This goes in the back matter.

VARIABLES: The infographics visualize the variables (height, duration, who texted, etc.)
