In 2015, I moved to Portland. I had no job, no friends, no car, and no life. And so, I started going on OkCupid.
Turns out there's no limit to how many people you can message at once. I was waking up at like 3 p.m. I would spend my entire day on my roommate's computer. Messaging 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 guys. Message, message, message, message, message, message, message, message. Do you want a date? And then I would go on dates. The guys didn't care that I didn't have a job. They didn't care that I didn't have a car. They'd come pick me up at my house. Or I'd meet them at the cafe across the street. They didn't really care about anything except that I was a woman. And so, in 2015, I met approximately 50, no, 55, no, 65. The number keeps climbing. 75 guys on OkCupid and Tinder combined. Hobbies? Going on OkCupid. Source of income? OkCupid. Something else? Something, something. I started documenting my dates, every little detail. What they were wearing, who paid. And since I had to listen to it, the entire story of how their ex was totally crazy and it was totally their fault that they broke up.
