# Pill spots — three :60 parody pharma commercials

Fictional-pill-commercial chat, Aug 2026. Three fake prescription-drug ads,
written in the real FDA-ad grammar (warm VO, b-roll, accelerating
side-effects crawl, "ask your doctor" closer). Storyboard frames S1–S6 per
spot live in `frames.json`; boards drawn at gpt-image-2 · medium · 1536x1024
for Sophie's review BEFORE any animation pass.

## SOMNIVEX® — "Be there."
*Dream-placement therapy. Appear in the dreams of someone you miss.*

**:00–:10 · S1 — kitchen window, dusk.** Woman holds a framed photo of her
grandmother.
> VO (warm, low): "You still think about her. The porch. The lemonade. The
> way she hummed while she watered the ferns. What if tonight… you could
> visit?"

**:10–:20 · S2 — product on the nightstand, moonlight.**
> VO: "Somnivex is the first dream-placement therapy. One tablet before bed,
> and you appear — gently — in the dreams of someone you miss."

**:20–:28 · S3 — she falls asleep, serene.** Curtains breathe.

**:28–:42 · S4/S5 — the dream.** Porch swing, lemonade, fireflies; then the
two of them dancing under an enormous moon.
> VO: "Tell her the news. Ask her the recipe. Stay as long as the REM cycle
> allows."

**:42–:55 · side-effects crawl (accelerating), over the dream:**
> "Somnivex may cause drowsiness, which is the point. Do not take Somnivex if
> you are unsure the other person misses you back. Appearing in the wrong
> person's dream occurs in one in eight users; apologize and exit through any
> door. Grandmothers may retain a lingering sense of being visited. Do not
> operate heavy machinery inside someone else's dream. Dreams involving exes
> require a separate prescription and a long talk with your therapist."

**:55–:60 · S6 — end card.** SOMNIVEX wordmark over twilight and fireflies.
> VO: "Somnivex. Be there."
> SUPER: *Ask your doctor if being missed is right for you.*

## PLACEBREX® — "It's working because you think it is."
*Active ingredient: nothing.*

**:00–:12 · P1/P2 — the perfect ad.** Wheat field at golden hour; golden
retriever catches a frisbee through a sprinkler rainbow.
> VO (impossibly warm): "Life is full of moments. Placebrex moments."

**:12–:22 · P3 — dawn kayak on a glassy lake.**
> VO: "Placebrex doesn't ask what's wrong. It doesn't need to know. Because
> Placebrex contains… nothing. And nothing works."

**:22–:32 · P4 — product shot.** Matte white bottle: *active ingredient:
nothing.*
> VO: "In a double-blind study, Placebrex performed exactly as well as
> Placebrex."

**:32–:42 · P5 — backyard dinner, string lights, everyone laughing.**

**:42–:55 · side-effects crawl:**
> "Placebrex is not indicated for anything. Side effects are whatever you
> expect them to be. Do not take Placebrex with a generic placebo, as they
> are the same thing and you will have paid twice. If you forget to take
> Placebrex, it still works. Individual results are entirely individual.
> Placebrex is available wherever you believe it is."

**:55–:60 · P6 — end card.** PLACEBREX wordmark on warm cream.
> VO: "Placebrex. It's working because you think it is."
> SUPER: *Ask your doctor. Watch them sigh.*

## OBLIVANE® — "Watch it again. For the first time."
*Erases the plot of one show of your choosing. Just one. You pick.*

**:00–:10 · O1 — the finale is over.** Man alone in TV glow, credits
rolling, remote hanging from his hand.
> VO: "The finale's over. You'd give anything to watch it again — for the
> first time. Now you can."

**:10–:20 · O2 — product by the remote and popcorn.**
> VO: "Oblivane targets and erases the plot of one show of your choosing.
> Just one. You pick."

**:20–:32 · O3 — the plot leaves quietly.** He sleeps in the recliner;
ribbons of TV-static light drift out of his head like fireflies leaving.
> VO: "While you sleep, Oblivane gently escorts every twist, every theory,
> every podcast recap out of your memory."

**:32–:42 · O4/O5 — rediscovery.** The streaming menu says NEW TO YOU; then
the couch gasp, popcorn in the air.
> VO: "That gasp? You've made it four times."

**:42–:55 · side-effects crawl:**
> "Oblivane may also remove which friend recommended the show; they will
> remind you. Do not use Oblivane on shows with fewer than two good seasons.
> Mild grief may occur when it ends. Again. Spoilers no longer affect you;
> smile politely. In rare cases users have forgotten the show exists and
> experienced a perfect Tuesday. Oblivane is not approved for erasing your
> browser history, your twenties, or the movie Cats, though we understand."

**:55–:60 · O6 — end card.** Couple silhouetted before the glowing screen.
> VO: "Oblivane. Watch it again. For the first time."
> SUPER: *Ask your doctor to name their show. Everyone has one.*
