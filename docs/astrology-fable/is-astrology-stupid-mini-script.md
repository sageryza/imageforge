# Is astrology stupid? — the boys, the girls, and the cage
## A Seedance 2.0 Mini draft script, 2026-09-19

**Source — her own words, two places, 2026-07-28:** the voice memo
*"Astrology Perspectives Discussion"* (`2026-07-28_1425_2026-07-28T21_25_25Z`,
1:40, the boy contingency · the girls · the Libra · the spaghetti date · the
convergence) and the message she dictated into the Imprint chat the same
hour that carried the part the memo lost (*"is it stupid or is it bad" ·
Christians afraid · boys scared because they can't do it · "crazy" as a way
of containing people · mental institutions*). The VO below is lifted from
those two verbatim, cut into clip-sized lines; nothing is paraphrased. Her
own picture of the girls (*"two girls gabbing over a dinner on high back
stools over wine and appetizers like cheese and crackers, and above their
heads twelve circles each with a different color and a different person
inside"*) is the girls' set.

**The pipeline, per the standing rules:** Seedance 2.0 **Mini**, **480p,
3:4** (560×752), **through Atlas** (never APIFRAME for Mini), 4–15s a clip,
main shots only. **NOTHING HERE IS SENT** — every clip waits for her "go"
for that exact job, and the exact prompt + references go on file the
moment one is sent (`forge-video-jobs`). The VO is hers (the memo itself,
or a TTS read on `eleven_multilingual_v2`) and is laid under the cut
afterwards, so every prompt says *no dialogue*.

**Continuity — how the same people stay the same people across clips.**
Mini takes a still with a person in it, so the plan is WAVES: the first
clip of each set is shot from a description (no reference exists yet),
then ONE frame is pulled from it and rides as `[Image1]` on every later
clip of that set — named by its slot only, never described (the
never-describe-a-reference rule). Four such stills carry the whole film:

- **the crowd** — a frame from clip 1 → clips 2, 9, 11
- **the two girls** — a frame from clip 3 → clips 4, 10, 13
- **her alone** (a crop of the girl on the left in that same frame) →
  clips 5, 6, 7, 8, 11
- **him** — a frame from clip 5 → clips 6, 7

So the shooting order is: wave 1 = clips 1, 3 · pull frames · wave 2 =
clips 2, 4, 5, 10, 13 · pull his frame · wave 3 = clips 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12.
What NO reference carries is written in the prompt (her red dress on the
date, the bare white room); what a reference carries is not.

**Cost, estimated:** 13 clips, ~97 seconds, at Mini's Atlas rate (~1.1¢/s)
≈ **$1.10** for the whole draft; a redo of one clip is 5–15¢.
