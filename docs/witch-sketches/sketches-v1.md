# Witchcraft comedy sketches — v1

Sophie's brief (2026-09-03): very short, 8–20 seconds, played completely
straight. Eight sketches as dictated; text kept verbatim. Her marks live on the
deck page "Witch sketches v1" in the `witchcraft-comedy-sketches` chat.

## 1. Crystal Employee Performance Reviews
TEXT: Annual Crystal Performance Reviews
YOU: "Amethyst. Sit down."
Amethyst on table.
YOU: "You've had a very strong year in brand awareness."
pause
YOU: "But I'm having trouble identifying what you actually do here."
cut to amethyst
YOU: "And 'vibes' is not a department."

## 2. Extremely Low-Stakes Spell
TEXT: A spell to make someone else cancel the plans
Very serious shot of you lighting a candle.
YOU: "You will need one black candle, rosemary, a small piece of paper, and the sincere desire to stay home."
Write something. Sprinkle herbs.
YOU: "Fold the paper away from you three times."
Phone buzzes.
TEXT MESSAGE: "I'm so sorry can we rain check 😭"
You stare into camera.
YOU: "Do not abuse this power."

## 3. Witchcraft OSHA
You enter frame holding clipboard.
YOU: "Hi. Witchcraft OSHA."
Inspect altar.
"Open flame next to dried herbs." — write
"Unlabeled jar of brown powder." — write
"Human tooth." — pause
"Unlabeled human tooth." — write
"That's gonna be a citation."
Note: could be a recurring character auditing increasingly unhinged customer-submitted altars.

## 4. Magical Object Adoption Agency
Tiny strange object sitting on table.
YOU: "This is Gerald."
"Gerald is approximately 80 years old."
"He does well with cats."
"He does not do well with mirrors."
beat
"We don't know why."
TEXT: Gerald is available for adoption.

## 5. Crystal Performance Review: Rose Quartz
YOU: "Rose Quartz, your numbers are incredible."
Rose quartz.
YOU: "Everyone knows who you are. Everyone wants you."
"But customer feedback is mixed."
pause
"Apparently several people are still single."
longer pause
"Do you want to explain that?"

## 6. Witch Infomercial
Overly distressed reenactment.

"Men?" — more devastated
"Emails?" — even worse
"Knowing that everyone you love will someday die?"
HARD CUT to cheerful product shot.
"Try A Little Bag of Rocks™."

Quick shots:

## 8. Advanced Witchcraft
You carefully arrange candles, crystals and herbs.
TEXT: Advanced manifestation technique
Very ceremoniously place your keys on top of the thing you need to remember tomorrow.
YOU: "Now it is impossible to leave the house without encountering the intention."
beat
"This is one of the most powerful spells I know."
