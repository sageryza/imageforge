# Standalone diary comics

One-off illustrated comics from Sophie's ideas, drawn through the house
diary-comic style ref (`refs/movie-style.jpg`) via gpt-image-2.

## "The urge to share"
A six-panel comic about the compulsion to overshare online: she sits with
cookies and milk, resists the SHARE button, walks toward the door — then runs
back and clicks it anyway, and the screen bursts into comics of her whole life.

- `urge-to-share-a.webp` — softer, cleaner ink
- `urge-to-share-b.webp` — scratchier cross-hatch, richer final panel
